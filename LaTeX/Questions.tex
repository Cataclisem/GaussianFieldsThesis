		It is clear $v(\omega) = 0$ for $\omega \in T_x \bigcap V^{*}$ since $v$ is harmonic and depends on the values of its neighbours.
		
		So we need to check the points in $T_x \setminus V^{*}$. Now since $T_x \bigcap V^{*}$ is dense in $T_x$, then for any $\omega \in T_x$ we have that there exists a sequence $x_n \in T_x \bigcap V^{*}$ such that $x_n \to \omega$.
